# Substitution checklist

## Before purchasing

- Record the exact original yarn variant, ball mass, length, colour and dye lot.
- Read the pattern's finished gauge, stitch, size and care requirements.
- List which properties must match: dimensions, handle, opacity, colour availability or care.
- Choose a candidate using documented specifications, not the weight-category name alone.
- Calculate metres separately for each colour. Round each colour's replacement ball quantity up separately.
- Keep testing and project-specific extra metres explicit. Do not treat zero extra metres in an example as a recommendation.

## Paired record

- Identify both yarns, colours and lots and the person working the samples.
- Record needle size and material, stitch and any changed conditions.
- Make samples large enough to measure a central area away from edges.
- Record dimensions and stitch/row counts before care and after completely drying.
- Follow applicable label care and record what was actually done. Differing care is a condition difference, not something to conceal.
- Record sample mass only if measured with a suitable scale; leave unavailable measurements blank.
- Keep photographs, measurement date, acceptance condition and result with the samples.

## Interpreting a result

A label gauge is a manufacturer reference. A hypothetical calculation is not measured consumption. One knitter's paired record applies to those documented conditions; it does not prove universal equivalence or long-term wear. Report a failed test as faithfully as a passing one.
